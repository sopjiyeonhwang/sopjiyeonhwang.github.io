About

A voice of bright, coloratura light.

Dr. Jiyeon Hwang is a Korean lyric coloratura soprano known for her distinctive vocal range and bright, sweet timbre, performing extensively in operatic roles, recitals, and concerts across the United States, Italy, the Czech Republic, and South Korea.
